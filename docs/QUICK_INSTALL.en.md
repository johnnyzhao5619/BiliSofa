# Install BiliSofa in five steps

[简体中文](QUICK_INSTALL.md) · Updated 2026-10-26

**No compilation required. Download the WGT and let a desktop installer sign it locally for your TV. Your computer can be turned off during everyday viewing.** The package requires Tizen 9.0. Compatibility with other versions is not established.

1. **Download the app and installer.** Get the [BiliSofa 0.66.0 preview WGT](https://github.com/johnnyzhao5619/BiliSofa/releases/download/v0.66.0/BiliSofa-0.66.0-unsigned.wgt), and Apps2Samsung for [Windows](https://github.com/Apps2Samsung/Apps2Samsung/releases/download/v2.8.3/Apps2Samsung-v2.8.3-win-x64.msi), [Mac Apple silicon](https://github.com/Apps2Samsung/Apps2Samsung/releases/download/v2.8.3/Apps2Samsung-v2.8.3-macos-arm64.dmg), [Mac Intel](https://github.com/Apps2Samsung/Apps2Samsung/releases/download/v2.8.3/Apps2Samsung-v2.8.3-macos-x64.dmg) or [Linux](https://github.com/Apps2Samsung/Apps2Samsung/releases/tag/v2.8.3). Compare the WGT hash with `SHA256SUMS.txt` in the [release](https://github.com/johnnyzhao5619/BiliSofa/releases/tag/v0.66.0), using `shasum -a 256 filename.wgt` on Mac or `Get-FileHash .\filename.wgt -Algorithm SHA256` in PowerShell. The [0.65.1 baseline](https://github.com/johnnyzhao5619/BiliSofa/releases/tag/v0.65.1) is available separately.
2. **Enable TV Developer Mode.** Connect the computer and TV to the same trusted LAN. Open TV **Apps → App Settings**, enter **12345**, set Developer Mode **On**, enter the installing computer's LAN IP and restart the TV. Menus vary by firmware; follow the [Samsung device guide](https://developer.samsung.com/smarttv/develop/getting-started/using-sdk/tv-device.html).
3. **Select your TV and Custom WGT.** Open Apps2Samsung, connect to your TV and select the downloaded `BiliSofa-0.66.0-unsigned.wgt` under its Custom WGT option. BiliSofa does not need to appear in the community catalogue. Labels may differ by installer version.
4. **Sign locally and install.** Follow the installer's Samsung account and TV certificate prompts, signing in on Samsung's official page. Samsung TV requires a valid signature; unsigned does not mean signature-free. Keep your Author certificate for updates and retain application identity `BiliSofa01.TV`. Do not send certificates, passwords, DUIDs or cookies to the maintainer. For an Author mismatch, recover your previous certificate before considering uninstalling and losing local data. See [Samsung certificate requirements](https://developer.samsung.com/smarttv/develop/getting-started/setting-up-sdk/creating-certificates.html).
5. **Open the app on the TV.** In **0.66.0**, enter **`www.bilibili.com`** (or `bilibili.com`) to enable the packaged service profile. Arbitrary websites are not supported; do not enter credentials or tokens. Version 0.65.1 has no website step. Start as a guest, then optionally sign in by scanning with your official Bilibili app. Use arrows, OK and Back. Turn off your computer when done installing.

Apps2Samsung is an independent third-party tool, not bundled here. Its [v2.8.3 documentation](https://github.com/Apps2Samsung/Apps2Samsung/blob/v2.8.3/README.md) describes custom WGT signing / installation; **this BiliSofa installation route has not been independently tested**. The 0.66.0 preview passed compilation and static package checks but has not completed TV runtime, installation or upgrade acceptance. An [official-tool installation guide](INSTALLATION.md) is also available.

If the TV is missing, check the LAN, computer IP, Developer Mode, restart and router isolation. For signing errors, check certificates and the original Author before uninstalling. Installation success does not establish playback or casting compatibility. In [Issues](https://github.com/johnnyzhao5619/BiliSofa/issues), provide only the app version, TV model, firmware and redacted errors, without QR codes, private addresses, credentials, certificates or captures.

The author provides the app free for learning and testing, with no profit-making activity at present. Programme content comes from Bilibili and its delivery infrastructure; online playback still uses buffering and caches. Entering a website or self-signing does not remove platform terms risks.
